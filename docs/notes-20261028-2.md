PortableTAK 2026.10.28.2 (test release): fixes doubled audio on OpenMANET talk groups from a node.

**Fix**
- **OpenMANET talk groups:** on an OpenMANET node the server's link to the talk groups sent every packet twice, because the node's Wi-Fi bridge has two addresses (10.41.0.1 and 10.41.250.10). Radios on the talk group could hear doubled or garbled audio from the server. It now sends once per network interface.

**TAK PTT plugin (installed separately, not part of this release)**
- Transcripts on the phone: with TEXT ON in HISTORY, each transmission shows what was said. Talk-group transmissions the phone hears are turned into text too, also without the server. It uses Android's on-device speech recognition (Android 13 or later), without internet; nothing is sent anywhere.
- The guide's voice chapter has a new Transcripts paragraph.

**Tested:**
- On Node01 (OpenMANET node, this drive) and Node02 across the mesh:
  - talk group to server: Node02 sent on CH 1, Node01's server recorded it in LOBBY;
  - Node01 to Node02 on CH 2: 273 of 273 packets;
  - server to talk group, after the fix: 207 packets on CH 1, all distinct, all from Node01.
- Transcripts on a Samsung S22: recorded clips from Node01 and a live talk-group transmission.
- Fresh-drive build test: 7 of 7 services.
- Not yet tested: real ManetNode radios on the talk groups, two phones, Bluetooth headsets.
